Who We Are

About Asha Education Trust

Asha Education Trust is committed to empowering young minds with industry-relevant skills in the healthcare and elevator technician sectors. We provide quality training that equips students with the technical and practical knowledge needed for employment.

Our mission is to bridge the gap between education and industry by offering hands-on learning and job placement, ensuring our students become skilled professionals ready to contribute to their respective fields. We aim for their brighter future by earning a decent living for themselves.

  • ✓ 7 years of experience conducting skill-based vocational trainings.
  • ✓ Approved by National Skill Development Corporation (NSDC) under Ministry of Skill Development & Entrepreneurship.
  • ✓ Approved by Healthcare Sector Skill Council (HSSC).
  • ✓ Equipped with AC Multimedia Classrooms and latest practical equipment.
  • ✓ More than 700 candidates successfully trained and employed in leading industries.
  • ✓ Extensive experience in CSR Project implementation for sustainable livelihood generation.
Our Programs

Specialized Training Disciplines

🩺

Nursing Courses

Comprehensive nursing foundation courses including ANM, GNM, B.Sc. (Nursing), Post Basic B.Sc. (Nursing), and CMSED certification pathways.

🔬

Paramedical Degree & Diploma

Professional qualifications in Medical Laboratory Technology (MLT), Radiology, Operation Theatre Technology (OTT), Cardiac Care (CCT), Physiotherapy, and Hospital Management.

🏥

Allied Healthcare Training

Practical quick-employability paths: Emergency Medical Technician, Nursing Assistant, Dental Assistant, and Hospital Front Desk Executive roles.

⚙️

Lift Mechanic Technician

Specialized technical training covering escalator and elevator maintenance, electronic control boards, wiring safety standards, and structural repairs.

🚨

First Responder & First Aid

Essential life-saving emergency training modules, cardiorespiratory resuscitation (CPR), triage, and immediate trauma support standards.

CSR Projects Execution

Corporate Partnerships

CSR Project Accomplishment & Registrations

Asha Education Trust stands amongst the frontrunner CSR executing agencies in northern India, bringing accountability, structured vocational planning, and verified employment data to corporate CSR contributions.

  • 1 Registered under Income Tax Act Section 80G & MCA for corporate CSR allocations.
  • 2 Covered under Section 2 of Schedule VII of the Companies Act 2013 targeting education and employment-enhancing vocational skills.
  • 3 Successfully completed over 10 CSR projects for leading national companies, including ONGC and HDFC.
  • 4 Focus areas include children, women, elderly, differentially abled individuals, and general rural livelihood development.
