Scholarship Support Programs

Lifekare Medical and Technical Institute is committed to providing financial assistance to deserving candidates. We believe that financial constraints should never stand in the way of high-quality vocational and paramedical education.

  • ✓ Fully sponsored corporate seats available for meritorious applicants from low-income families.
  • ✓ Up to 100% tuition fee waiver based on entrance screening scores and interview performance.
  • ✓ Special scholarship support schemes for candidates from rural areas and underrepresented social indicators.
  • ✓ Admissions alignment with Government schemes as per the eligibility criteria of the candidates.
