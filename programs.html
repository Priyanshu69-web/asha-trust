Explore 15+ Professional Pathways

ANM - 2 Years

Auxiliary Nurse Midwifery (ANM)

Approved by Indian Nursing Council and Haryana Nurses Council. Focused on basic nursing care, community health, and clinical diagnostics.

GNM - 3 Years

General Nursing & Midwifery (GNM)

Approved by INC & HNRC. Comprehensive program training students in general bedside care, maternity nursing, and surgical assistance.

B.Sc - 4.5 Years

B.Sc Nursing

Approved by Indian Nursing Council. Focuses on advanced clinical practices, pharmacology, nursing management, and healthcare research.

Lateral - 2 Years

Post Basic B.Sc Nursing

Tailored for GNM diploma holders looking to upgrade their skills and qualifications to a professional nursing degree.

OTT - Diploma

Operation Theatre Technology

As per NCAHP guidelines. Specialized training in pre/post-operative surgical setups, sterilization, and anesthesia support.

Radiology Tech

Radiology & Medical Imaging

Focuses on diagnostic technologies including X-Ray, CT Scan, MRI scan handling, patient positioning, and safety guidelines.

MLT Tech

Medical Lab Technology (MLT)

Covers biochemistry analysis, clinical pathology tests, hematology setups, blood banking processes, and lab reporting procedures.

Dialysis Tech

Dialysis Technology

Prepares specialists to configure hemodialysis machines, monitor patients during dialysis, and maintain dialysis equipment.

Cardiac Tech

Cardiac Care Technology

Learn cardiovascular monitoring, ECG procedures, cardiac ICU setups, and medical catheterization lab assistance.

Physiotherapy

Physiotherapy

Acquire skills in electrotherapy, kinesiology, muscle rehabilitation exercises, heat therapies, and post-injury management.

Management

Hospital Management

Focuses on hospital billing operations, administration, database management, front desk, and general healthcare operations.

Vocational

Lift Mechanic Technician

Specialized engineering course covering escalator maintenance, elevator motor repair, panel controls, and safety wiring.

First Aid

First Aid Certification

Hands-on skilling in CPR, bleed control dressings, shock management, burns dressing, and accidental trauma care.

Emergency

First Responder Training

Prepares students for quick disaster evacuation, basic triage protocols, medical emergencies response, and spine board splinting.

Refresher

Upskill Training Modules

Short-term refresher programs for working nursing assistants and technicians to learn advanced modern hospital practices.
