About LMTI

Bridging the gap between classroom education and technical industry requirements to prepare students for professional success and sustainable livelihood generation.

Pioneering Skill Development for India's Future

Asha Education Trust (AET) is a Public Charitable Trust registered with the Office of the Sub-Registrar, Delhi in 2005. The Trust is a Registered NGO in DARPAN Portal of Niti Aayog, Govt. of India. AET, with its philanthropic initiatives in the field of social service through education, continues to hand-hold and shape the lives of many children and young adults – both women and men, especially those from economically underprivileged and vulnerable families.

Lifekare Medical and Technical Institute (LMTI), located at Gurugram, is a constituent of Asha Education Trust. LMTI imparts vocational career training and skill development primarily in Allied Healthcare & Engineering Sector.

Our Mission

To uplift and empower underrepresented youth, rural candidates, and low-income individuals by providing fully-sponsored and affordable technical vocational skills. We aim to convert raw aptitude into professional excellence, building a sustainable livelihood generation system.

Our Vision

To serve as the benchmark institution for vocational skilling in India, driving social impact through strategic corporate Scholarship Support alliances. We envision an ecosystem where every student, regardless of socio-economic status, has direct access to high-standard education and immediate career employment.
