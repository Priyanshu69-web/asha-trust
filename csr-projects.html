Education and Employability : Our Commitment to Society

At Asha Education Trust, we believe that true education goes beyond classrooms and certifications. It is about creating opportunities, restoring dignity, and enabling individuals to build a better future for themselves and their families.

As a not-for-profit organization, we are committed to contributing to society through initiatives that support education, skill development, employability, and community welfare. Our philanthropic efforts are guided by a simple belief: when people are empowered with knowledge, skills, and opportunities, they become agents of positive change in their families and communities.

Our Initiatives Include:

🎓

Supporting economically disadvantaged youth in accessing quality skill training.

🧭

Providing career guidance and counselling to help young people make informed career choices.

🤝

Collaborating with industry, educational institutions, NGOs, and corporate partners to expand opportunities for skill development and employment.

📢

Organising awareness programmes, workshops, and community outreach initiatives focused on education, employability, and life skills.

🌟

Encouraging a culture of volunteerism, mentorship, and community participation through our students, alumni, faculty, and partners.

💚

Supporting community initiatives that promote education, health, environmental awareness, and the well-being of underprivileged sections of society.

We also believe in giving back to society through acts of compassion and social responsibility. From time to time, we support community initiatives that promote education, health, environmental awareness, and the well-being of underprivileged sections of society.

Ripple Effect of Employment

Every student who secures meaningful employment through our efforts creates a ripple effect—strengthening families, inspiring communities, and contributing to the nation's economic and social development.

Empowerment Through Philanthropy

For us, philanthropy is not just about providing assistance; it is about creating lasting opportunities that enable people to become self-reliant, confident, and employable.

Building an Empowered Society

Together with our partners, donors, volunteers, and well-wishers, we remain committed to building a more inclusive, skilled, and empowered society.

Proven Excellence in CSR Execution

Lifekare Medical and Technical Institute (LMTI), a constituent of Asha Education Trust, is a registered implementation partner for CSR projects across India. We successfully bridge the gap between corporate CSR budgets and real-world social impact by training and employing candidates from economically underprivileged background indicators.

  • ✓ DARPAN Registration: Asha Education Trust is a registered NGO in DARPAN Portal of NITI Aayog, Govt. of India.
  • ✓ Tax Exempted Status: Fully registered and compliant under IT Act Section 12A & 80G for corporate donations.
  • ✓ Schedule VII Compliant: Covered under Section 135 & Schedule VII of the Companies Act 2013, promoting employment-enhancing vocational skills among children, women, and the differently-abled.
  • ✓ Completed Projects: Successfully completed over 20+ CSR projects for top PSUs, banks, and MNCs with 1200+ candidates trained and placed.
